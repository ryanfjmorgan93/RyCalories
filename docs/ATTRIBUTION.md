# Attribution

Iron ships exercise demonstration images and instructions from third-party sources. This file
records the attribution required by their licences. The same list is exported as `ATTRIBUTIONS`
from `src/data/exerciseDemos.ts` for a Settings → About screen.

## Exercise images — Workout Guide (Bryl Lim)

The three-frame exercise images under `public/exercises/<slug>/` (generated at build time by
`scripts/exercise-media.mjs` from the `@bryllim/workout-guide` npm package) are created and
curated by [Bryl Lim](https://bryllim.com) and are licensed under
[CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/).

Seventy-six first-pose frames are rasterised adaptations of artwork from
[Everkinetic](https://github.com/everkinetic/data), also licensed under
[CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/). The source artwork was placed on
a transparent canvas, recoloured for monochrome display, and exported as optimised PNG, then
resized to WebP by this app's build. Exact per-frame source URLs are recorded in
`node_modules/@bryllim/workout-guide/manifest.json`.

CC BY-SA 4.0 requires that adaptations be distributed under the same licence, that credit be
given, and that changes be indicated. This file, together with `ATTRIBUTIONS` in
`src/data/exerciseDemos.ts`, is that credit; the changes are described above and in the manifest.

Full licence text: <https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/legalcode>

## Exercise instructions — free-exercise-db

The written step-by-step instructions in `src/data/exerciseInstructions.json` (generated by
`scripts/fetch-instructions.mjs`) come from
[free-exercise-db](https://github.com/yuhonas/free-exercise-db), which is released into the
public domain under the [Unlicense](https://unlicense.org/). No attribution is legally required,
but the source is credited here for transparency.
